StayOnMap: Find your home on the map

Renting in India still runs through brokers, listing sites full of duplicates, and photos that rarely match the flat. StayOnMap is a broker-free rental map, live across seven Indian states, where owners list for free and tenants contact them directly.

The homepage is the map, on web and on mobile. You don't scroll through a feed. You pan to the neighbourhood you care about and the homes there appear as pins. You can filter by budget, BHK, furnishing and area, open a listing, chat with the owner, and book a visit without leaving the app.

Every listing carries a Trust and Risk score, built from reviews, reports and owner verification, so you can judge whether a home and its owner are real before you visit.

Under the hood

StayOnMap covers six kinds of property: apartments, houses, land, PGs, shops and short stays. Each kind is asked the questions that fit it, so a plot is never asked about bedrooms and a short stay is booked by the night. Owners can list through a guided wizard on web or mobile, or simply by chatting on WhatsApp.

Each listing also shows neighbourhood intelligence: what's nearby, how you'd get around, and what kind of place it is. Every fact is labelled measured, derived or estimated, and anything we don't know is shown as missing rather than guessed. The product is a React web app and an Expo Android app sharing one Node.js and PostgreSQL backend, in a jade-green design system built around the map.
