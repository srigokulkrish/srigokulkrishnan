Cosmonus: Building Real-World Intelligence

Most software understands text. Very little of it understands a street, a home, or a group of people deciding what to do this evening. Cosmonus is the company I am building to close that gap.

We are a small team building for streets, homes and the people in them, and we start with problems, not technologies. We look at something ordinary, like finding a place to live or deciding what to do tonight, and ask why it still works the way it does. Could it be simpler? Could it understand more about where you are and who you are with? Could the technology disappear into the experience?

Then we build. Some ideas become products, some become research, some stay experiments. All of it lives under one roof, so what we learn in one place makes the others better.

What Cosmonus is made of

Studio: the creative practice behind every product, covering web, animation, image generation and video. Products: StayOnMap and Happenous, both built on knowing where you are. Intelligence: spatial intelligence and Trust Score. Agents: systems that turn understanding into finished work. Research: what we are learning, published as we go.

The visual identity is deliberately quiet: a bold geometric wordmark, a neutral palette, and real photography of the physical world instead of stock imagery, because the subject is the world outside the screen.
