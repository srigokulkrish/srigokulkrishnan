Happenous: Your next plan is already happening

Most social apps keep you on the screen. Happenous is built to get you off it. It is an activity-first social network for meeting people in real life, now live on Google Play across twenty Indian cities, with iPhone coming soon.

Runs, badminton, coffee, photo walks: someone hosts a real Activity at a real place and time, and you can see what people near you are actually doing, today or this weekend. Others ask to join, the Host accepts them into the Crew, and they meet. Checking in with the Host's six-character code proves you were there, and that unlocks everything after it: reviews of the people you actually met, Moments from the Activity, and Recognition over time. Buddies form only after you've done something together. There are no one-way follows.

The question it answers is simple: what can I actually do today? Its loop is just as simple: do, complete, improve, connect, and do again. It rewards real-world action, never time spent in the app.

Designed around trust

A location is two locations. Until a Host accepts you, an Activity shows only an area about 3 km across. The exact meeting point and the Crew chat open only to the accepted Crew.

Attendance is the anchor. Check-in turns "accepted" into "was there", and only people who were there can review each other. Nothing claims more than it can prove: badges come from recorded events, and review rates stay hidden until enough people have written one. The words are part of the design too. It says Activity, Host, Crew and Moment, never event, organiser or post.
