01Off the screen, on purpose

Most social apps are designed to keep you on the screen. Happenous is designed to get you off it. It's an activity-first social network for meeting people in real life: runs, badminton, coffee, photo walks. You see what people near you are actually doing, ask to join the Crew, and go.

The question it answers is simple: what can I actually do today? It rewards real-world action, never time spent in the app.

02How it works

Find an ActivitySee what's Happening around you, today or this weekend, as a list or on a map.
Join the CrewAsk to join. Once the Host accepts, the exact meeting point and Crew chat open.
Show upMeet the group and check in with the Host's six-character code.
Keep goingReview the people you met, share Moments, and earn Recognition.

03Designed around trust

A location is two locations

Until a Host accepts you, an Activity shows only an area about 3 km across. The exact meeting point is visible only to the accepted Crew. Discovery never touches the real address.

Attendance is the anchor

Check-in turns "accepted" into "was there", and only people who were there can review each other. The check-in shows someone had the Host's code during the Activity. Happenous is honest that it runs no ID or background checks.

Nothing claims more than it can prove

Badges come from recorded events, and review rates stay hidden until enough people have written one. Buddies form only after you've done something together. There are no one-way follows.

04Words are part of the design

The vocabulary is load-bearing and used the same way in the app, the website and the code. Choosing it was a design decision, because each word sets an expectation about how people relate to each other.

Activitynot event
Hostnot organiser
Crewnot attendees
Buddynot follower
Check-innot attendance
Momentnot post
Happeningnot explore
Recognitionnot leaderboard

05Design system

White is the ground everywhere, separated by whitespace and a single hairline, never by tinted panels. There is one brand colour, Happenous Orange, and it appears at most once per screen: on the primary button, the active state, or a count. There are no gradients anywhere in the system.

  • Outfit for headings and the wordmark, Inter for everything else, on a short type ladder from 11px to 32px.
  • Radii of 16 for cards and sheets, 12 for images and 8 for inputs, with pill-shaped buttons.
  • Motion in three steps (120, 200 and 320 ms), plus a 480 ms "celebrate" duration reserved for Buddy connections and milestones. It's never used for penalties, and reduced motion is respected everywhere.

06Under the hood

An Expo and React Native app with expo-router, an Express and TypeScript API on PostgreSQL with PostGIS for the location rules, a React moderation console for administrators, and a static marketing site. Everything runs on Google Cloud.
